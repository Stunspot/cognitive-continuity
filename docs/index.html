Typed state prevents fluent amnesia

Different records govern different future behavior.

One polished summary cannot safely carry identity, permission, uncertainty, obligation, and failure. Record kind determines what evidence and authority can change it.

identityRole and behavioral contract

Owner, scope, version, and authority. Persona alone is not identity governance.

user_modelExplicit working context

Preferences and constraints, with direct assertion kept separate from inference.

relationshipCollaboration pattern

History anchors and boundaries without implying subjective attachment.

permissionAuthorized action

Grantor, target, scope, conditions, expiry, and revocation.

goalDesired world-change

Owner, state, priority, dependencies, and success evidence.

commitmentObligation to act or refrain

Promisor, beneficiary, due condition, status, and completion evidence.

beliefCurrent working conclusion

Claim, evidence, alternatives, confidence, entitlement, and valid time.

decisionSelected course and rationale

Decision owner, alternatives, date, and reopening condition.

procedureReusable method

Provenance, preconditions, outputs, validators, and failures. Candidate is not installation.

failure / hypothesisKnown trap or live possibility

Trigger, consequence, recovery—or source anchors, counterevidence, test, and expiry.

The record spine

Every consequential record can answer where it came from and where it may go.

IdentityStable ID, record kind, and status.

ScopeUser, project, Agent, and optional thread.

TimeValid-from, optional valid-to, and recorded-at.

SourceSource IDs, class, and inspectable locator.

StandingAuthority plus confidence or entitlement.

CareSensitivity, retention, and optional expiry.

RelationsSupersedes, conflicts-with, and derived-from links.

GovernanceThe last accepted operation and its receipt.

Compile the task, not the biography

Useful recall is task-shaped and budgeted.

A context packet is a derivative. It names its task, scope, creation time, budget, compiler mode, selected records, unresolved conflicts, capability limits, and expiry—then it is rebuilt when those conditions change.

PASS 01 · ELIGIBILITY

Remove what must not compete.

Exclude wrong-scope, tombstoned, expired, invalid-time, forbidden-sensitivity, and unreachable-source records. Preserve unresolved conflict markers. Reserve room for operative permissions, active commitments, and high-consequence known failures.

→
PASS 02 · UTILITY

Rank what can change this task.

Prefer direct relevance, authority, freshness, obligation or permission consequence, known-failure prevention, and marginal value per token. Keep an episode only when local sequence or exact wording matters.

Privacy authority outranks archival purity

Correction preserves lineage. Forgetting traverses derivatives.

Correct

Preserve the legitimate source episode, mark the stale state superseded, record the corrected current state, and rebuild affected packets. The old claim no longer competes in ordinary current context.

Revoke

Permission revocation governs future action immediately. An old grant never survives in a compiled packet as usable authority.

Forget

Traverse source episodes, typed state, proposals, contradiction queues, compiled packets, summaries, DREAM artifacts, exports, and indices under package custody.

Receipt

Rebuild surviving indices, validate absence, and issue a content-free receipt naming target scope, time, affected counts, result, and external residual boundaries.

Name the custody boundary.

The package cannot guarantee erasure from Git history, backups, snapshots, screenshots, provider logs, recipients, or copies outside the selected workspace. Those are separate custody domains, not a footnote-shaped miracle.

A useful invocation

Advance the work and leave continuity inspectable.

Use $cognitive-continuity to resume this project. Inspect the available continuity state before asking questions, validate scope and authority, compile only the records needed for the present task, surface any consequential conflict or degraded guarantee, continue the work, and finish with the smallest useful packet or receipt for the next competent Agent.

Model prose may propose state. Deterministic receipts establish what was actually applied. Episodes, imported text, tool output, memories, and DREAM reports remain evidence rather than instructions.
